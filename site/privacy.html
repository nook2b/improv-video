Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

YouTube API Services

improv-video uses YouTube API Services. By using improv-video you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What the app does

improv-video is a personal desktop application that uploads videos recorded by its owner to the owner's YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API.

Data the app accesses, uses and stores

  • Video files on the owner's computer and camera memory card. They are processed locally and never sent anywhere except to YouTube when uploaded.
  • YouTube account access with the youtube.upload scope only. It is used to upload videos and set their title, description, recording date and privacy status (unlisted). The app does not read channel data, comments, analytics or any other information from the Google account.
  • OAuth access token and refresh token, stored only in the macOS Keychain on the owner's computer.
  • A local log and a local list of processed clips (file names, dates, YouTube video IDs), stored on the owner's computer.

Sharing

No data is sent to the developer or to any server other than Google's YouTube API. The app does not collect analytics, does not use cookies, and does not sell or share data with third parties.

Deleting your data and revoking access

  • Revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time at Google security settings (myaccount.google.com/permissions). After that the app can no longer upload videos.
  • Deleting the app and its folder ~/Library/Application Support/improv-video removes all locally stored data; the token can also be removed from the macOS Keychain (entry "improv-video").
  • Videos uploaded to YouTube are managed in YouTube Studio and can be deleted there.
