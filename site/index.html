improv-video

improv-video is a personal desktop tool for macOS. It copies clips from an Insta360 camera memory card, edits them into one video per shooting day (color, brightness, audio clean-up) and uploads that video to the owner's own YouTube channel as an unlisted video to share with the team.

The app runs only on the owner's computer. It is not distributed to other users.
